zkLane

Private micropayments for AI agents on Zcash.

One shielded Zcash transaction opens a channel. Every payment after that is a real zkChannels zero-knowledge proof that the merchant verifies without learning who paid, how much is left, or which payments belong together. One shielded transaction refunds the rest.

What's real, what's trusted

  • Real: zkChannels payments (libzkchannels: CL signatures, Pedersen commitments, range proofs), revocation and cheat detection, shielded Zcash funding and refund via zingo-cli over the Nym mixnet.
  • Trusted: escrow. The merchant holds the funds, because Zcash can't yet enforce channel closes on-chain. A Transparent Zcash Extension is the roadmap.
  • Revealed to the merchant: payment amounts and timing, channel size at open and close, and the customer's IP (zkChannels runs over plain TCP).
